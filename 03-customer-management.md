# Customer Management (CM)

## Purpose
CM provides the customer-service workflow from customer discovery through service, billing, engagement and resolution.

## Functional Flow

### 1. Find & View
Capabilities:
- Global search
- Customer and account lookup
- Address / premise view
- Account overview
- Account balances

### 2. Service & Orders
Capabilities:
- Create service order
- Assign services and equipment
- Appointments and scheduler
- Trouble tickets and stages

### 3. Billing & Payments
Capabilities:
- Billing inquiry
- AR balances
- Take payment
- Bill-cycle processing

### 4. Engage & Resolve
Capabilities:
- Notes and alerts
- Marketing and promotions
- Inbox and activity
- AI summaries through Mimir

## End-to-End Outcome
A service agent should be able to handle the customer lifecycle — search, service, billing and follow-up — from one unified web workspace.

## Relationship to AM
CM manages customers and accounts. AM tracks the physical and service inventory tied to those customers/accounts.

Service orders in CM drive equipment and network provisioning in AM.

## Implementation Guardrail
Do not infer additional customer workflows or business rules from these headings alone. Detailed behavior must come from requirements or existing implementation.
